# Matrix Room Descriptions for SFB 1252 Space

## 🏛️ SFB General

**Room Name:** `SFB General`  
**Alias:** `#sfb-general:uni.koeln`

**Description:**
Welcome to the main gathering space for SFB 1252! This is our central hub for introductions, important announcements, general discussions about the Collaborative Research Centre, workshop coordination, and anything related to our research community. Whether you're new to the group, have questions about upcoming events, or want to share news relevant to the entire SFB, this is the place to start.

**Topics include:**

- New member introductions and welcomes
- SFB 1252 announcements and updates
- Workshop schedules and event coordination
- General research discussions
- Community news and updates
- Questions about the SFB structure and activities

---

## 📊 Research Data Management

**Room Name:** `Research Data Management`  
**Alias:** `#sfb1252-rdm:uni.koeln`

**Description:**
Dedicated space for all things related to Research Data Management (RDM). Share best practices, discuss tools and workflows, ask questions about data handling, storage, and archiving. Whether you're struggling with GDPR compliance, looking for the right data repository, or want to share a great new tool you've discovered, this is your go-to room for RDM expertise and support.

**Topics include:**

- Data management planning (DMP)
- Data storage and backup strategies
- Data sharing and repositories
- GDPR and privacy considerations
- RDM tools and software recommendations
- Data documentation and metadata
- Version control for research data
- Long-term preservation strategies
- Workshop follow-up discussions

---

## 🐱 Katzentisch

**Room Name:** `Katzentisch`  
**Alias:** `#katzentisch:uni.koeln`

**Description:**
Welcome to the "kids' table" of SFB 1252! This is our designated space for off-topic conversations, coffee break chat, memes, random thoughts, and everything that doesn't quite fit into serious academic discourse. Share funny research memes, talk about your weekend plans, post pictures of your office cats, or just decompress from the serious business of saving science. Remember: even the most dedicated researchers need a place to be silly!

**Topics include:**

- Coffee break conversations
- Memes and funny content (research-related or not)
- Off-topic discussions
- Personal updates and life outside research
- Random thoughts and observations
- Pet photos (especially cats, given the room name)
- Weekend and vacation stories
- Anything that makes you smile

---

## Room Settings Recommendations

### 🏛️ SFB General

- **Permissions:** All members can post and read
- **Moderation:** Standard moderation for announcements
- **Pinned Messages:** Welcome message, community guidelines, current workshop schedule
- **Notifications:** Medium priority

### 📊 Research Data Management

- **Permissions:** All members can post and read
- **Moderation:** Light moderation, encourage helpful discussions
- **Pinned Messages:** RDM resources, useful links, workshop materials
- **Notifications:** Medium priority

### 🐱 Katzentisch

- **Permissions:** All members can post and read
- **Moderation:** Very light moderation, fun atmosphere
- **Pinned Messages:** "Rules: Have fun, be kind, share memes"
- **Notifications:** Low priority (optional for members)
