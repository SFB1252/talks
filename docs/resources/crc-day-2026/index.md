## Overview

Session presented at the CRC 1252 Day 2026, covering Project S support options in three parts:

- **Publishing Research Data** — licensing your data, FAIR principles, data journals, and choosing a repository (Zenodo, OSF, LAC, and more)
- **MediaPipe SIG** — a recap of the group and a call for contributions
- **AI Good Practice** — an announcement for the AI Ethics workshop at the upcoming CRC 1252 retreat (5–7 October 2026)
