# Large Language Models for Linguistic Analyses: Applications and Limitations

The SFB/CRC 1252: Prominence in Language organized a two-day workshop on "Large Language Models for Linguistic Analyses: Applications and Limitations" on November 24-25, 2025, at the University of Cologne.

The workshop explored the use of LLMs as tools for linguistic research, covering applications like automatic annotation, text analysis, and stimulus generation, while also critically examining their cognitive plausibility and inherent biases. It featured invited speakers, a poster session, and discussions on methodological challenges and best practices.
