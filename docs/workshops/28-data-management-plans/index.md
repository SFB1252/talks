## Overview

Writing a data management plan (DMP) is often an unpopular task. However, funding agencies are placing increasing emphasis on the inclusion of a detailed plan in grant proposals. A multitude of workshops and resources seems to have turned writing a DMP into almost a science of itself. This session aims to take a practical approach, focusing on the key aspects that define a good DMP in terms of both content and format, as well as how to use it to effectively support your own research.

## Learning Objectives

- Understand what a bad DMP is
- Understanding what content is important in a DMP
- Recommendations for software to support implementation
- Raising awareness for the relevance of DMPs
