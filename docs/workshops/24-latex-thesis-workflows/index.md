## Overview

This follow-up session looks at how LaTeX scales to longer academic documents
and collaborative writing. The focus is on practical workflows for theses,
bibliographies, and modular document organization.

## Learning Objectives

- Organize larger LaTeX projects for theses and dissertations
- Manage bibliographies and cross-references effectively
- Understand collaboration workflows with Git and Overleaf
- Recognize common LaTeX problems and basic debugging strategies

## Topics Covered

- Structuring larger documents across multiple files
- Bibliographies, citations, and cross-references
- Shared templates and collaborative editing
- Troubleshooting common errors in longer projects
