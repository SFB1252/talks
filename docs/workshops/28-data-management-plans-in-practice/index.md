## Overview

Creating practical, funder-aligned data management plans for research projects, including documentation, storage, sharing, and archiving.

Data Management Plans (DMPs) are increasingly required by major funding agencies such as the DFG and the European Research Council. Beyond compliance, a well-crafted DMP serves as an operational roadmap for research projects, saving time and preventing data loss throughout the research lifecycle.

## Topics Covered

- **Funder Requirements & Guidelines:** Understanding expectations from the DFG, EU Horizon Europe, and institutional policies
- **Core Components of a DMP:** Data description, formats, volume, metadata standards, access rights, and legal/ethical compliance
- **DMP Tools & Templates:** Hands-on walkthrough of tools such as RDMO (Research Data Management Organiser) and DMPonline
- **Storage, Backup & Security:** Defining day-to-day data storage protocols and access controls during the active project phase
- **Long-Term Archiving & FAIR Data:** Planning for repository deposit, persistent identifiers (DOIs), and public access licenses
