## Overview

Fundamentals of research data management (RDM). Best practices for data
collection, organization, storage, and sharing throughout the research
lifecycle.

## Learning Objectives

- Principles of research data management
- Data management planning
- Tools and platforms for data storage and sharing
- Legal and ethical considerations in data management
