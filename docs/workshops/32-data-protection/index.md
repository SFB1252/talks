## Overview

Handling sensitive data with GDPR-aligned workflows, secure sharing via Sciebo, and encryption basics.

Linguistic and behavioral research often involves personally identifiable information (audio/video recordings, participant names, demographics, eye-tracking data). This workshop provides practical, actionable protocols for complying with GDPR and university data protection requirements without hindering your collaborative research workflow.

## Topics Covered

- **GDPR Fundamentals for Researchers:** Personal data, sensitive categories, legal bases for processing, and participant rights
- **Informed Consent & Data Processing Agreements:** Crafting compliant consent forms and understanding data handling responsibilities
- **Secure Storage & Sharing with Sciebo:** Working with university-approved cloud storage, link expiration, password protection, and access permissions
- **Encryption in Practice:** File-level and container-level encryption using open-source tools (7-Zip AES-256, VeraCrypt, GnuPG)
- **Anonymization & Pseudonymization:** Practical techniques for de-identifying transcripts, corpora, and experiment outputs before sharing or archiving
- **Incident Response:** What to do in the event of accidental data exposure or lost devices
